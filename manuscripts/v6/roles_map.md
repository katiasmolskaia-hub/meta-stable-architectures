# Roles Map for the Next Architecture

## Field

The field is not a commander.

It functions as:

- a living diagnostic surface,
- a carrier of global pattern,
- and a structure in which gatherability or loss of gatherability becomes visible.

## Instructor / RC

The instructor reads the field and helps organize response.

Its role is:

- to interpret field pattern,
- to direct attention,
- to support route-sensitive return,
- and to avoid replacing the field with hard control.

## Agents

Agents are not passive targets.

They are:

- local carriers of state,
- holders of weak ties,
- sources of both destabilization and stabilization,
- and the material from which the field reorganizes itself.

## Bonds

Bonds are not just background edges.

They should become explicit process elements:

- weak,
- adaptive,
- stress-sensitive,
- and potentially memory-bearing.

## Memory

Memory should move beyond storage of isolated values.

The next architecture should distinguish:

- field memory,
- route memory,
- and temporary crisis-bond memory.

## Recovery / Re-entry

Recovery is not merely "the crisis ended."

Re-entry means:

- the field becomes gatherable again,
- the return path fits the route by which the crisis was entered,
- and the resulting configuration remains alive rather than over-constrained.
