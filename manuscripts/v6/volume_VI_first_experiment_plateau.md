# Volume VI First Experiment Plateau

## Why this file matters

The early Volume VI work has now reached a first meaningful experimental plateau.

This file records what has already been established by the first minimal bond-route experiment, so that the next steps can proceed from a stable base rather than from scattered impressions.

## What was tested

The first experiment did not attempt to reproduce the full inherited architecture from Volume V.

Instead, it tested one narrower question:

- can different `crisis routes (маршруты кризиса)` produce different `bond-state trajectories (траектории состояний связей)` and different `re-entry patterns (паттерны повторного входа)`?

The tested routes were:

- `drift route (маршрут дрейфа)`,
- `shock route (маршрут удара)`,
- `distortion route (маршрут искажения)`.

The minimal bond variables were:

- `bond viability (жизнеспособность связи)`,
- `bond freedom (свобода связи)`,
- `bond stability (устойчивость связи)`,
- `distortion load (нагрузка искажения)`,
- and `re-entry support (поддержка повторного входа)`.

## What is now established

The first experiment already supports several nontrivial claims.

### 1. Bond-state transitions are not empty decoration

The route logic does not collapse into one generic crisis response.

Different routes now produce different bond outcomes.

### 2. Distortion route is already qualitatively distinct

`Distortion route (маршрут искажения)` consistently drives the system toward:

- high `distortion load (нагрузке искажения)`,
- collapse of `re-entry support (поддержки повторного входа)`,
- and final `distorted bonds (искаженным связям)`.

This is already a strong separation result.

### 3. Shock route produces a recoverable support response

`Shock route (маршрут удара)` now behaves as an acute but recoverable regime.

At the crisis peak, the bonds can enter a `crisis-support (кризисно-поддерживающий)` configuration.

By the end of the trajectory, they settle into `stabilizing bonds (стабилизирующие связи)`.

### 4. Drift route differs from shock mainly through the tail

`Drift route (маршрут дрейфа)` does not currently collapse like distortion, but it also does not recover in the same way as shock.

Its strongest distinguishing feature is now the `recovery tail (хвост восстановления)`.

The drift case spends much longer in a weakened post-crisis regime and remains closer to `restored weak bonds (восстановленным слабым связям)` than to fully stabilizing bonds.

## Why the tail result matters

The most important result of the latest refinement is that `drift (дрейф)` and `shock (удар)` are better distinguished by `tail-sensitive diagnostics (диагностикой хвостов)` than by final scalar values alone.

This means that:

- route diagnosis should not rely only on final recovery level,
- and the temporal aftershape of recovery may be one of the central observables of Volume VI.

In particular, the following measures became useful:

- `recovery time to threshold (время возврата к порогу)`,
- `tail area (площадь хвоста)`,
- `re-entry tail area (площадь хвоста повторного входа)`,
- and `time in restored-weak regime (время в режиме восстановленных слабых связей)`.

## What has been passed already

At this stage, the project has already passed several important checkpoints:

- a minimal formalization of Volume VI exists,
- bond-state interpretation rules exist,
- a first executable route experiment exists,
- distortion has become a clearly distinct regime,
- shock and drift are no longer identical,
- and tail diagnostics has emerged as a meaningful discriminator.

## What remains for the next round

The next step is not to make the model much larger.

The next step is to make the state logic more explicit in time.

In practice, this means:

- tracking when transitions occur,
- not only where each route ends,
- and measuring the sequence of bond-state changes during and after crisis.

## Minimal conclusion

The first Volume VI experiment has reached a real plateau.

It does not yet prove the whole architecture.

But it does show that:

- route-sensitive bond dynamics can be made operational,
- the transition logic is not empty,
- and recovery tails may be one of the key signatures that distinguish crisis routes.
